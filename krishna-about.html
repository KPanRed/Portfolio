Philosophy

"I use research as a lens into the human condition — revealing the signals and tensions that often go unnoticed."

Ethnography Human Behavior Systems Thinking Empathy Mixed Methods Strategy

I'm a Senior User Experience Researcher with nine-plus years of exploring the emotional and cultural forces behind human behavior. My work is rooted in ethnography and a deep respect for people's lived experiences — studying behavior in context to surface the stories, motivations, and patterns that shape how people move through the world.

I look for the deeper frictions that influence how people think, decide, and connect — the tensions most teams overlook. Addressing these leads to experiences that feel intuitive, human, and genuinely impactful.

By blending qualitative depth with quantitative structure, I ensure insights don't just resonate — they drive decisions. My work helps teams move beyond assumptions and build with empathy at the center.
